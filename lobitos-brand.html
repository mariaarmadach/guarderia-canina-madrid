01 — The Idea

Not a kennel.
A clubhouse.

Lobitos Social Club is a members-only retreat where dogs are greeted by name, lounge in good company, and go home tired and happy. We borrowed the warmth of an old sporting club — dashed-line stamps, checkerboard floors, a touch of brass — and pointed it entirely at the four-legged.

Lobitos is simply the name — the regulars, the locals, the good ones who keep coming back.

Membership Creed

“Be a good boy. Be a good girl. Be exceptionally well-rested.”

Commission — A Note for the Painter

The mascot,
painted by hand.

The heart of the identity is a hand-drawn club “member” — a very good dog dressed for the occasion, in a loose, confident single-weight line. It becomes the primary mark and rides on the cards, signage, totes and tags.

Two characters to choose from — or do both as a matched set:

GlamourDachshund in a headscarf, cat-eye shades & pearls.
SportingPointer in a bucket hat & tied neckerchief.
Technique
Loose single-weight line. Confident, a little wobble — the hand should show, not vector-perfect.
Ink
One colour — Club Navy (#1F2D4A) on Bone (#F4ECD8). No shading or fills.
Crop
Front-facing bust, centred — sits inside a circular badge.
Deliver
Vector (SVG / AI) + transparent PNG, plus a reversed one-colour version.
Moodboard — tone & world
Reference — the two members & the line
